# Code of Conduct

## Our Community

Cyber Wolf Dash has community level sharing at its core — the game is built to be
played, made, and remixed together. We want the community to be a place where creators
of every skill level can share levels and get constructive feedback.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Be kind and constructive, especially to first-time level creators
- Give feedback on levels, not on the person
- Credit others' work (music, templates, remix bases) when you build on it
- Keep it family-friendly: the game is priced and positioned for a general audience

**Not tolerated:**
- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- Stolen or republished levels/music presented as your own
- Malicious content in shared levels (crashes, exploits, shock content)
- Spam, advertising, or selling levels outside official channels

## Scope

Applies to the project's GitHub spaces (issues, discussions, PRs) and, later, in-game
level sharing and official community channels. Creators who publish levels are bound by
the same standards.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content and suspend community features (level publishing,
comments) for violations. Report issues to the maintainers via GitHub or the SOETech
LNM contact channel. Repeated or severe violations result in a permanent ban from
community features.
